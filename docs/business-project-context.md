# MomentoVino: business & product context

Context brief for creative work (ads, App Store screenshots, social posts, landing pages, emails). Paste it whole, or the sections you need, into a design or copy prompt.

## In one line

**MomentoVino keeps every wine moment (the bottle, the people, the place, the photos) on a world map you can go back to, and shares it with your family and friends.**

It is a memory app built around wine, not a wine-rating app.

## The problem we solve

You had an incredible bottle on a trip last summer, and today you can't remember its name. Your wine memories are scattered: blurry photos lost in the camera roll, an Instagram caption, a napkin. When a friend asks *"what was that wine?"*, you go blank. Families buy the same bottle nobody liked, again. The bottles that marked birthdays, trips and dinners slowly fade.

## The promise

Every time a bottle is opened at a moment that matters, you save **the night, not just the wine**: who was there, where it happened, the photos, how much you loved it. Each moment becomes a pin on your personal globe. Your family and friends add their moments too, so the memories live together. Years later you can spin the globe and go right back to that table.

## Who it's for

- **Wine lovers, not wine experts.** People who enjoy a good bottle and want to remember it. No jargon, no scores, no sommelier knowledge needed.
- **Couples and families who mark life with wine**: anniversaries, holidays, Sunday lunches, the bottle saved for a special date.
- **Travelers** who visit wine regions (Tuscany, Mendoza, Douro, Bordeaux, Napa) and want a keepsake of the journey.
- Skews 30+, comfortable and warm rather than technical. Many users are 50+ couples and multi-generation families.

## What the app delivers

### 1. Save the moment
The core object is a **moment**: a title, the date, the place, up to 3 photos of the night, an optional 1 to 5 star rating, a short note about who was there and what made it special, and the bottles that were opened (up to 8 per moment). The location fills itself in from GPS, or you search for any city.

### 2. Pin it on your world map
Every moment becomes a pin on an interactive 3D globe, the **wine atlas**. Counters show how many moments, countries and wines you have collected. Spinning the globe is the emotional payoff: your life told through the places where wine brought people together.

### 3. Add the bottle in seconds
Point the camera at a label, or pick a photo from the gallery, and AI identifies the wine: name, producer, region, country, style, plus a one-line description. No typing. Every bottle also lands on your personal wine shelf, which you can search and which groups duplicate bottles automatically.

### 4. Share with family and friends
Create a family group with a name, description and cover photo, and invite people by username or by email. Everyone adds their own moments and bottles, and you can see each member's moments, countries and wines. It is one shared space for the people you drink with.

### 5. Made for everyone
The app is available in English, Portuguese (Brazil and Portugal), Spanish and Italian, including the AI wine descriptions and emails.

## How a new user experiences it

1. Welcome: a slowly spinning globe and *"Keep every moment in a world you can go back to."*
2. Two quick questions about why they're here and what they struggle with (forgotten bottles, lost photos, going blank when asked).
3. They create their **first moment**, then add the bottle by scanning its label.
4. The moment appears on their globe as the first pin: *"One moment, one bottle. The first pin on your map."*
5. A free trial starts, and they create an account so their moments are safe.

## Why it's different

Wine apps like Vivino, Delectable and CellarTracker are **databases**: ratings, prices, cellar inventory, expert notes. MomentoVino is **personal and emotional**: the story around the bottle, the people and the place. It doesn't tell you what to buy. It helps you remember what you lived.

## Business model

- iOS app (iPhone and iPad). Android is not live.
- Subscription, **MomentoVino Pro**:
  - **Monthly: $2.99/month with a 3-day free trial** (the default plan)
  - **Annual: $24.99/year** (about $2.08/month, about 30% off)
  - Cancel anytime in Settings.
- Prices are shown in each user's local currency by the App Store.

## Brand voice

- **Warm, intimate, unhurried.** Like a friend toasting at the table, never a sommelier lecturing.
- **Emotion first, feature second.** Lead with the memory, then show the feature that makes it possible.
- **Short, concrete scenes** beat abstractions: *"the bottle we opened at grandma's 80th"* beats *"track your wines"*.
- **Vocabulary to use:** moment, memory, the night, the people, the place, world, map, globe, atlas, pin, share, family, friends, go back to, relive, keep.
- **Avoid:** "journal" and "diary" (retired wording), "inventory", "cellar management", "ratings database", wine-snob jargon (tannins, terroir lectures), scores and points.
- **Punctuation:** no em dashes (—) in user-facing copy. Use commas, periods or colons.

## Visual identity

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Wine | `#722F37` | Brand color, headlines, primary buttons, tab bar |
| Ink | `#3F2A2E` | Body text |
| Terracotta accent | `#C2703E` | The "Vino" in the wordmark, highlights, icons |
| Cream | `#F5EBE0` | App background |
| Brown | `#5C4033` | Secondary buttons |
| Muted | `#6E5A5E` | Secondary text |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Cards |

- **Wordmark:** "Momento" in Wine plus "Vino" in Terracotta, set in a serif.
- **Type:** DM Serif Display for headlines and numbers, DM Sans for body and UI.
- **Shapes:** soft rounded cards (12 to 20 px radius), pill buttons, soft shadows.
- **Signature visual:** a thin-line wireframe globe in wine red, with small wine-glass pins.

### Creative style (App Store screenshots and ads)

- Deep burgundy gradient background with a soft warm glow behind the headline.
- Two-line serif headline: first line in cream, second line in peach italic (for example *"Save the night, / not just the wine."*), with an optional light gray subtitle in two short lines.
- A real app screen inside a phone frame, tilted slightly to the right, its bottom running off the canvas.
- **Photography:** warm, golden-hour, candid and genuinely happy. People toasting at a table outdoors, vineyards and cypress trees, family lunches, couples aged 50+, multiple generations. Never studio or stock-perfect; it should feel like a memory.
- **Avoid:** cold blues, nightclub or party energy, wine as luxury status, heavy drinking, minors holding drinks.

## Facts to keep straight

- Do **not** claim wine recommendations, discovery, shopping, prices, or a public social feed. None of those exist.
- Do **not** claim Android availability.
- The AI identifies wines from the **label** (camera or gallery photo), not from taste.
- A family group is **private** and joined by invitation.
- Moments can exist with or without photos. The rating is optional.
